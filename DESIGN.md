## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative precision, foresight, and algorithmic trust. Built for high-stakes enterprise project governance, the aesthetic reflects mission-critical clarity: information density managed through strict spatial rhythm, dark-field optical hierarchy, and calibrated luminescence. 

The visual style blends **Corporate Modernism** with subtle **Technical Glassmorphism**. Dark slate-navy planes establish deep structural stability, while targeted, low-attenuation glows (indigo, cyan, and status hues) surface predictive intelligence and critical risk states without visual fatigue. Surfaces feel physical yet weightless, defined by hairline boundaries, controlled optical blur, and surgical micro-details.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for prolonged operational focus in deep dark-mode environments.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B1020`)**: Deep void navy; anchors the entire interface canvas.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#111827`)**: Structural sidebar, top bar, and inset regions.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#151D2E`)**: Standard card container, data panels, and module backgrounds.
- **Surface Elevated (`#1E293B`)**: Dropdowns, active popovers, and elevated modal panels.

### Brand Accents
- **Primary Indigo (`#6366F1`)**: Directs user flow, signals AI synthesis, primary actions, and confirmed workflows.
- **Secondary Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`)**: Denotes real-time velocity, active telemetry, machine learning inferences, and dynamic tracking.

### Semantic Tiers
- **Success Emerald (`#22C55E`)**: Stable risk index, resolved blockers, and healthy velocity.
- **Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Predictive anomalies, moderate risk factors, and deadline drift.
- **Danger Rose (`#EF4444`)**: Critical blocking events, severe risk thresholds, and compliance violations.

### Typography & Structure
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`)**: High-contrast, sharp typographic legibility for values, titles, and active inputs.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`)**: Mid-frequency metadata, table headers, inactive tabs, and supporting notes.
- **Borders & Dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)**: Hairline boundary standard across all surface perimeters.

## Typography

Typography balances geometric structure with tabular precision. 

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers clear, contemporary framing for dashboards, KPI groupings, and module titles. Tight negative letter-spacing ensures titles remain anchored and authoritative.
- **Body & Controls (Inter)**: Employs neutral grotesque metrics optimized for screen legibility across large data tables, event logs, and status readouts.
- **Numerical Data & Metrics**: Uses tabular figures (`tnum`) across all numeric output, risk probabilities, and timelines to prevent jitter during real-time data streaming.
- **Caps & Micro-Labels**: Set in uppercase Inter with wide tracking (`0.04em`) to designate status tags, column keys, and system states with immediate scanning efficiency.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a rigid 8px spatial grid combined with a responsive 12-column fluid framework.

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates multi-pane layouts: collapsible navigation bar (240px fixed), primary metric canvas, and modular risk feeds.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Secondary detail drawers collapse into slide-over panels or modal sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Metric strips convert to horizontally swipeable carousels; complex data tables drop to prioritized stacked card structures.
- **Rhythm**: Internal card padding uses `space-lg` (1.5rem) on desktop and `space-md` (1rem) on mobile. Micro-gaps between label and value pairs consistently rely on `space-xs` (0.25rem) or `space-sm` (0.5rem).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse gray dropshadows; it relies on structured luminous planes, tonal layer staging, and precise optical glass borders.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#0B1020`. Absolute background plane.
- **Level 1 (Docked/Inset Panels)**: `#111827` with a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Tiles)**: `#151D2E` with a `1px` perimeter stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Subtle backdrops apply `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` when floating over underlying charts.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Hover / Active Focus)**: `#192238` with border `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)` and a micro-glow shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Flyouts, Menus & Overlays)**: `#1E293B` featuring a crisp rim border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` accompanied by deep occluding elevation: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Telemetry Glows**: High-severity items emit low-radius aura effects (e.g., Critical Risk card emits `0 0 16px -2px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The interface maintains a precision-milled geometry using standard medium radius configurations (Option 2).

- **Micro Components (Pills, Status Badges, Toggle Markers)**: Fully rounded (`9999px`) to visually separate metadata markers from interactive controls.
- **Standard Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Select Boxes)**: `0.5rem` (8px). Maintains crisp structural discipline.
- **Containers & Panels (Cards, Modal Windows, Metric Tiles)**: `1rem` (16px) for external contours. Internal child compartments step down to `0.5rem` (8px) to maintain harmonious concentric radii.
- **Hairline Strokes**: All boundary lines are fixed at exactly `1px` with no rounding offsets.

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#6366F1`, text `#F8FAFC`, font weight 600. Subtle inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`). Hover: background `#4F46E5`, box-shadow `0 0 16px rgba(99,102,241,0.4)`.
- **Secondary (Ghost Glass)**: Background `rgba(255,255,255,0.03)`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`, text `#F8FAFC`. Hover: `rgba(255,255,255,0.07)`, border `rgba(255,255,255,0.16)`.
- **Destructive**: Background `rgba(239,68,68,0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(239,68,68,0.3)`, text `#EF4444`. Hover: background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Risk Indicator Chips**: Height `22px`, corner radius `9999px`, horizontal padding `8px`, typography `label-sm`.
  - *Critical*: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#EF4444`, border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`. Includes an animated pulsing dot indicator.
  - *Guarded*: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#F59E0B`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
  - *Optimal*: Background `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`, text `#22C55E`, border `1px solid rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.25)`.
- **Filter Tags**: Surface `#111827`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`, text `#94A3B8`. Active state shifts border to `#6366F1` and text to `#F8FAFC`.

### Form Controls (Inputs, Checkboxes, Radios)
- **Text Inputs**: Height `40px`, background `#111827`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`, corner radius `0.5rem`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focus state transitions border to `#6366F1` with an outer ring `0 0 0 2px rgba(99,102,241,0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Size `18px`, base `#111827`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.16)`. Selected state uses `#6366F1` background, crisp white glyph check, and a subtle indigo drop glow.

### Cards & Dashboards
- **Metric Cards**: Background `#151D2E`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`, radius `1rem`, padding `1.5rem`. Contains a top-row micro-label and status chip, center tabular metric (`28px`), and a bottom delta sparkline or trajectory trend indicator.
- **Lists & Data Tables**: Header row features uppercase tracking, text `#94A3B8`, bottom border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`. Alternating rows maintain `#151D2E` with hover highlighting to `#1A2337`.

### AI Risk Telemetry Widgets
- **Confidence Gauge**: Semi-circular or linear segment bar with gradient fills transitioning from `#06B6D4` to `#6366F1`.
- **Mitigation Action Row**: Embedded contextual card with left border accent (3px solid `#6366F1`), offering single-click approval controls for AI-suggested balance re-allocations.
